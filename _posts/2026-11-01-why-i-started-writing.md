This is a sample post so the home page, archive and tag pages have something
to show. Replace it with your own writing, or delete it.

Writing things down is one of the best ways to understand them. A blog is a
low-pressure place to do that in public: short notes, half-finished ideas and
project logs all count.

A few ideas for first posts:

1. A project you're working on and why
2. Something you learned recently that surprised you
3. A tool or workflow you can't live without
